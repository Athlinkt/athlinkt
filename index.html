Why Athlinkt Was Created

It started on a court. Competitive basketball from a young age — recruitment letters, AAU circuits, high expectations, and the kind of environments that shape you whether you're ready or not.

That early view from the inside showed what helps young people grow — and what quietly gets in the way. Division I ball came next, then coaching, then long-term leadership as a head coach.

Now add parenthood to the resume. Full-court press of perspective unlocked. Athlinkt is shaped by lived experience across sport, leadership, communication, and family dynamics.

The goal is simple: create tools and conversations that help people understand what to do, what to avoid, and how to build healthier environments for young people. No fluff. No filler. Just clarity.
