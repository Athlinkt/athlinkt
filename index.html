EVERY ATHLETE DESERVES CLARITY.

ATHLINKT exists to change that. Real tools, real data, real connection. Built for the athlete who has given everything to their sport.

Our Story

"This started on a court. It was built for every athlete who deserves more than a blank page after the sport ends."

ATHLINKT was built by someone who played D1, coached at the highest level, served as a Senior Woman Administrator, coordinated Title IX compliance, and raised athletes.

Athletes make some of the most important decisions of their lives with almost no real support. ATHLINKT is that support. Finally.
