About Athlinkt

Athlinkt is built to connect educators, parents, athletes, and leaders through modern, interactive tools that foster growth and collaboration.

This space is being created to support stronger relationships, clearer expectations, and a more thoughtful approach to development on and beyond the playing surface.

Why Athlinkt Was Created

The perspective behind Athlinkt comes from years of living this work from multiple angles.

It started with playing competitive basketball at a young age, followed by being heavily recruited, playing high school and AAU basketball, and traveling through environments filled with athletes, parents, coaches, and high expectations.

That journey provided an early view of what helps young people grow and what can get in the way. After competing at the Division I collegiate level, that experience continued into coaching and eventually into long term leadership as a head coach.

Now, with the added perspective of parenthood, Athlinkt is being shaped by lived experience across sport, leadership, communication, development, and family dynamics.

The goal is simple: create tools and conversations that help people better understand what to do, what to avoid, and how to build healthier, more effective environments for young people.

Core Focus Areas

Athlinkt is being built around practical, relevant content that helps people lead, respond, and grow with more clarity.

Development Mindset Parent POV

Content that helps families better understand growth, perspective, process, and what meaningful development can really look like over time.

Parent Behavior & Boundary Model

Guidance and tools that support healthier parent behavior, clearer boundaries, and stronger alignment between adults and the young people they support.

Coach Communication & Expectations

Resources that strengthen communication, clarify expectations, and help coaches create more consistent and effective environments.

Start Here

If you are a parent, begin with perspective, behavior, and boundaries.

If you are a coach, begin with communication and expectations.

If you are building around athletes, begin with development first and outcomes second.

Athlinkt will continue to grow into a place where these conversations become more practical, interactive, and impactful.
