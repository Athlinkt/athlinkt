TRANSFER PORTAL

Guidance + Support

The portal moves fast. The decision you make in 48 hours lives with you for years. ATHLINKT is the support system the portal doesn't come with.

Before you decide \u2014 think about this:

QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING

IF YOU GOT INJURED

If you suffered a season-ending injury tomorrow \u2014 would you still want to be at the school you're considering?

THE DEGREE

Does a degree from that school set you up for success after athletics? Your major matters beyond the sport.

YOUR TEAMMATES

Do you like the people who will be your teammates? Will they push you to be better? Can you see lifelong friendships?

YOUR DEVELOPMENT

Where will you be developed most \u2014 as a player AND as a person? Those aren't always the same place.

WINNING

Is winning important to you? If so \u2014 can you genuinely see the program you're considering winning?

THE COMMUNITY

Do you like the city, the campus, the culture? You'll be living there \u2014 not just playing there.

Your decision will impact the rest of your life. It's bigger than just the sport.

OUR STORY

"This started on a court. It was built for every athlete who deserves more than a blank page after the sport ends."

ATHLINKT was built by someone who played D1, coached at the highest level, served as a Senior Woman Administrator, coordinated Title IX compliance, and raised athletes. Not a tech company trying to figure out sports from the outside.

Someone who lived every stage \u2014 player, coach, parent, administrator \u2014 and saw the same gap, over and over. Athletes making life-changing decisions about the portal, about NIL, about what comes after \u2014 with almost no real support.

Not because nobody cares. Because nothing existed that was built specifically for them, by someone who understood them.

ATHLINKT is that thing. Finally.
